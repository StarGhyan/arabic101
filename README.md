# arabic101

An interactive Modern Standard Arabic learning platform designed for Windows, iPhone, iPad, and other devices.

## Learning experience

Learners study letters, vocabulary, grammar, and conversations alongside one another. Personal review repeatedly combines new and previously learned material.

- Arabic text, English meaning, consistent transliteration, and playable pronunciation for letters, words, and sentences.
- Adjustable support: hide transliteration or meanings, practice Arabic-only reading, or focus on listening.
- Alphabet instruction covering isolated, initial, medial, and final forms; joining rules; short vowels; syllables; sun and moon letters; and ta marbutah.
- Grammar through examples: gender, plurals, pronouns, possession, questions, prepositions, roots, nisbah, definiteness, adjective agreement, and sentence structure.
- Practice through matching, multiple choice, spelling, typing, sentence building, fill-in-the-blank, listening, and choosing conversational replies.
- Scene games with changing roles in classrooms, grocery stores, clothing stores, restaurants, workplaces, and homes. Map activities teach asking for and explaining directions.
- Daily vocabulary, category browsing, review of all learned material, and additional practice for mistakes.

## Vocabulary categories

Geography and directions; countries, continents, cities, streets, and planets; greetings and polite requests; family and relationships; school and university; careers and workplace responsibilities; numbers, arithmetic, percentages, dates, and time; food and drinks; animals and pet care; transport; colors; clothing; shopping; common expressions; and interactions with people in different social roles.

## Initial playable scope

1. Dashboard with words of the day and review.
2. Letter explorer with forms, sounds, and joining guidance.
3. Greetings, family vocabulary, and numbers.
4. Mixed practice with adjustable pronunciation and meaning support.
5. Classroom and grocery-store role-play scenes.

## Content and accessibility requirements

- Distinguish Modern Standard Arabic from dialectal speech in songs, films, and casual expressions.
- Treat English pronunciation guidance as an approximation; audio is the pronunciation reference.
- Support right-to-left Arabic, readable text sizes, keyboard navigation, visible focus, touch controls, and text alternatives to audio activities.
- Verify Arabic spelling, vowel marks, translations, and pronunciation before presenting content as authoritative.
- Use licensed or original media for song and film lessons.
